## 🔁 Retrospective Discussion

During our team retrospective, we reflected on several key aspects of our development process that we want to improve going forward:

### Emphasizing Issue Priorities
We acknowledged that not all issues carry the same level of urgency or importance. To address this, we will begin explicitly tagging and communicating the **priority level of each issue** (e.g., High, Medium, Low). This will help team members better manage their time, tackle high-impact tasks earlier, and avoid bottlenecks in project progress.

### Estimate vs. Actual Timings
To improve planning accuracy and personal accountability, we will start tracking the **estimated time to complete an issue vs. the actual time taken**. This will help us identify underestimations, overestimations, and better inform future sprint planning. Over time, this should enhance our ability to scope out features realistically and allocate resources more effectively.

### Improved Issue Tracking Workflow
We will be using a more structured **issue status workflow**:  
- `In Progress`: The issue is actively being worked on.  
- `In Review`: A pull request has been submitted and is waiting on peer review.  
- `Done`: The issue has been reviewed and successfully merged.  

This structure will keep our board cleaner and make it easier to monitor the state of development across the team.

### Reviewing at Least Two Pull Requests
To ensure everyone’s work receives feedback and no PRs are left behind, we’re asking each contributor to **review at least two pull requests per cycle**. This not only strengthens code quality but also helps team members stay familiar with each other's work, making it easier to collaborate and catch inconsistencies early.

### Require Multiple Reviews on PRs
Even for smaller changes, **pull requests must receive multiple reviews** before merging. This reinforces code integrity and keeps everyone aligned. It also prevents regressions or overlooked issues from slipping through. This policy will ensure that no piece of the codebase goes unchecked and helps foster a culture of shared ownership and quality assurance.

---

These changes aim to streamline our process, improve communication, and ensure the success of future sprints. Let’s keep this momentum going!
