# 🔁 Retrospective – Week of 5/20/2025

## What Went Well

- 🔧 **Code quality improved** through more consistent and detailed PR reviews.
- **Frontend styling** progressed significantly, with interactive elements like swipe animations and carousels implemented.
- **Cross-functional collaboration** between design and dev teams (e.g., Khang and Verania on animations).
- **CI/CD infrastructure** expanded with automated linting and formatting checks.
- Clear strides in **documentation** with Figma exports and page descriptions.

---

## What Could Have Gone Better

- **Simultaneous development** of scripts and UI led to miscommunication on logic placement.
- **Pipeline issues** created friction—some automated checks failed without clear errors.
- **Uncertainty around architectural decisions**, such as where validation logic should live and whether certain pages (like liked events) are in scope.
- Some team members experienced delays due to **merge conflicts** or awaiting approvals.

---

## Action Items

- **Clarify architectural expectations**: Define where validation, logic, and templating should live (frontend vs. backend).
- **Prioritize testing**: Implement and enforce unit test coverage for all new JS logic.
- **Improve PR process**: Consider setting up review guidelines and rotating reviewers for faster approvals.
- **Define feature scope**: Finalize the decision on features like the liked events page early in the sprint.
- **Investigate CI issues**: Schedule debugging of auto-trigger failures or consult mentors/office hours.
