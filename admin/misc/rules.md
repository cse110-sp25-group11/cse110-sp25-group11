# Group 11 Contract

## 1. Personal Goals

Each team member aims to:
- Improve their understanding while being in a team environment
- Contribute their part for their project.
- Being able to collaborate and communicate with their team

Achieving these goals will:
- Improve and grow the team bond
- Make sure that we are able to success towards our projects
- Making sure that team members are able to fairly contribute and not feel left out

## 2. Values, Mission, and Common Goals

**Core Values:**
- **Collaboration:** Even work distribution, mutual support, and shared effort.
- **Communication:** Transparent, timely updates (or feedback) via Slack and meetings.
- **Respect:** Inclusive, educational feedback; no one left out.
- **Organization:** Well-maintained documentation and clear processes.

**Mission:**
- To build an innovative campus event tracker app that helps students discover and organize events efficiently, using a swipe-based user interface.

**Common Goals:**
- Deliver a functional, user-friendly application by the project deadlines.
- Ensuring all team members contribute and learn throughout the process.
- Create a positive team culture.

## 3. Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths:**
- Diverse technical backgrounds (web, Docker, SSO, UI/UX, etc.).
- Leadership experience (Charles, Prasham).
- Strong communication and organizational skills.
  
**Weaknesses/Areas for Improvement:**
- Balancing workloads during busy academic workloads.
- Ensuring everyone is heard despite the large group settings.

**Addressing Weaknesses:**
- Regular check-ins to redistribute tasks as needed.
- Encouraging open, respectful criticsm and feedback.

## 4. People, Roles, and Accountabilities

**Team Leads:**
- Charles Nguyen: Project management, technical leadership, planning.
- Prasham Shah: Co-leadership, meeting facilitation, support.

**Developers:**
- Sachin, Wyatt, Lucas, Verania, Khang, Alan, Mia, Benjamin, Aryan: Software development, testing, documentation, and feature implementation.

**Logistical Responsibilities:**
- Meeting notes: Rotating among members present.
- Task creation and tracking: Managed via shared tools (e.g., GitHub Issues, Slack).
- Flexibility: Team members may cover for each other during absences or busy periods.

## 5. Needs and Expectations

**Needs:**
- Clear yet respectful communication.
- Timely updates on progress and blockers.
- Being able to help and ask for help.

**Expectations:**
- Attend meetings and participate actively whenever possible.
- Complete assigned tasks on time or communicate any potential delays early.
- Maintain organized, well-documented work (such as following coding style guidelines).

**Availability & Schedules:**
- Members will share their weekly availability at the start of each sprint.
- Risks due to time conflicts will be discussed and addressed as a team.

**Communication Strategies:**
- Primary: Slack for daily updates and quick questions.
- Secondary: Weekly meetings for planning and (TA) updates.

**Conflict Resolution:**
- Address issues directly and respectfully within the respective team.
- If unresolved, escalate to team leads for mediation.
- If unresolved via the team leads, the TA or Professor will step in

## 6. Rules and Activities

**Working Methods:**
- Have regular check-ins.
- Having code reviews and pair programming.
- Making sure the documentation and meeting notes are kept up to date.

**Measuring Success:**
- Completion of project milestones and deliverables using Github Issues.
- The growth and efficiency in both individual and team skills.

**Fair and Equal Contributions:**
- Regularly review workload distribution.
- Rotate less desirable tasks.
